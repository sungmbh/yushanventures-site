Automotive MicroLED strategy paper

A board-level strategic analysis of the automotive MicroLED ecosystem across display and non-display (lighting & sensing) applications — value-chain mapping from epitaxy to mass transfer, maturity and cost-curve assessment, and low-risk entry points such as adaptive driving beams, micro-projection HUDs, and "shy-tech" interior surfaces. Delivered as a make-vs-buy-vs-partner framework, competitor positioning, and a risk-adjusted 24-month roadmap for R&D and CAPEX decisions.
