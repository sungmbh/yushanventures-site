How we work

We run the work, not just the introductions.

First we agree on the decision you need to make. Then we research the options, speak with the relevant suppliers and experts, and turn the findings into a practical next step. Our reports separate confirmed facts, estimates and supplier claims.

✓ VERIFIED  ·  ◆ ESTIMATE  ·  SUPPLIER-STATED

Every figure carries its provenance.

Every chart is badged on its face: verified against a primary source, our own triangulation, or a vendor's claim recorded but unconfirmed. Where no honest figure exists we show a placeholder and say so.

Primary research, on the record.

Named expert interviews, supplier calls and trade-floor intelligence — a principal display engineer answering six written questions, a driver-IC house telling us the architecture everyone assumes is uneconomic at automotive screen size. Not a literature review with a logo on it.

Models you can edit.

Partner scorecards ship as working spreadsheets: weighted criteria, editable weights, ranking re-sorts when you change them. You get the model and the evidence tab behind every score, not just our conclusion.

A range when the answer is a range.

The biggest uncertainty in a business case is shown as a band, not a number. On one recent question the analyst view and the leading supplier's view were twofold apart. We published both, and named which decisions were safe inside either.

The story

How we got here.

Yushan built ecosystems, scouted technology for the world's largest industrial companies, advised venture funds, founded an accelerator — and the co-founder is back at the helm.

2011

Founded in Taipei — and lit the spark

Yushan Ventures established to bridge global innovation and Asia's ecosystems. Introduced Startup Weekend to Taiwan and ran the MobileMonday Taipei chapter. In these early years Yushan also made a handful of direct angel investments of its own capital into Taiwanese startups — most since wound down; Lucent Sky, a web-security company, remains.

MobileMonday Taipei
2012–2018

Co-built the APEC O2O Accelerator

Conceived and co-built the APEC O2O accelerator with Taiwan's government — running startup programs around the world, from Nanjing to Lima.

2015

Volkswagen Group, Audi & Coca-Cola

Became technology scouting partner to the Volkswagen Group and Audi — and introduced ProLogium, the Taiwanese solid-state lithium-ceramic battery maker, to Audi. Ran a China scouting engagement for Coca-Cola; toured 10 cities with GSMA on "The Rise of Asia's Innovation Machines".

2015–2016

VC partner to Malaysia's Smart Investment Facilitation

Appointed VC partner to the Smart Investment Facilitation programme, run with MDEC and Cradle Fund. Opened with an Investor Readiness Workshop in Kuala Lumpur for twenty Malaysian startups, then coached the five with the strongest fundraising potential through to term sheets.

Venture partner to HeidelbergCement's Foundamental fund; scouted vision tech in Japan for ABB's YuMi robot and high-temperature sensors for Siam Cement. Founded Mosaic Venture Lab in 2019, originally powered by Audi AG.
