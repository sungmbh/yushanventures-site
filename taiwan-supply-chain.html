Low-volume supplier sourcing

Make the first inquiry specific enough to answer.

A prototype run and a volume programme need different partners. We check the proposed quantity, process and schedule with the supplier instead of assuming a large manufacturer will accept a small order.

Prepare these inputs

  • A short product description and the application it must work in.
  • A bill of materials, with critical parts and acceptable alternatives marked.
  • Available drawings, design files and test requirements. Agree confidentiality before sharing sensitive files.
  • Prototype quantity, expected first order and later volume range.
  • Target dates, budget range and required quality or certification checks.
  • Who on your team can answer engineering questions.

If the design is not ready

Say what exists and what is still open. We can scope a partner search around that gap. Suppliers may need more detail before they can quote. A directory cannot resolve an incomplete specification.

What a quote needs to settle

Quantity, tooling or setup costs, lead time, test scope and the assumptions behind the price. These are questions for the supplier, not numbers we promise in advance.

Yushan's role

We are an advisory and scouting firm. We map the options, qualify potential counterparts and help both sides communicate. Your engineers own the design and technical acceptance. Manufacturers own their quotes, production and quality commitments.

What we do not promise

No guaranteed foundry allocation, minimum order, lead time, manufacturing price or production outcome. Access depends on the process, volume, readiness and capacity. Investor introductions may be possible; funding is never promised.
