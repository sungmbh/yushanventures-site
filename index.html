How we work

Evidence you can take to a board.

Industrial clients do not need another confident deck. They need to know which numbers survive scrutiny and which ones do not — so we label them.

✓ VERIFIED  ·  ◆ ESTIMATE  ·  SUPPLIER-STATED

Every figure carries its provenance.

A chart in our reports is badged on its face: verified against a primary source, our own triangulation, or a vendor's claim recorded but unconfirmed. A client should never mistake our estimate for a fact. Where no honest figure exists we show a placeholder and say so.

Primary research, on the record.

Named expert interviews, supplier calls and trade-floor intelligence. A principal display engineer answering six written questions. A driver-IC house telling us on a call that the architecture everyone assumes is uneconomic at automotive screen size. Not a literature review with a logo on it.

Models you can edit.

Our partner scorecards ship as working spreadsheets: weighted criteria, weights in an editable row, ranking re-sorts when you change them. You get the model and the evidence tab behind every score — one row of sourced fact per company — not just our conclusion.

A range when the answer is a range.

The biggest uncertainty in a business case is shown as a band, not a number. On one recent question the analyst view and the leading supplier's view were twofold apart. We published both, and named which decisions were safe inside either.

We run the room, not just the report.

Two-day strategy workshops at the client's site, in their language — SWOT built live with the leadership team, an anonymous employee survey fed back into the room, then minutes and roadmap written up as a formal report within the week.

Culture is a gating requirement, like the spec.

Before a German optics team entered a Korean Tier-1 programme we wrote the field guide alongside the technical one: how decisions actually get made, ISO 26262 and ASIL obligations, RACI for series production, ramp-up and spare-parts planning. Both gate the award.

The story

How we got here.

Yushan built ecosystems, scouted technology for the world's largest industrial companies, advised venture funds, founded an accelerator — and the founder is back at the helm.

2011

Founded in Taipei — and lit the spark

Yushan Ventures established to bridge global innovation and Asia's ecosystems. Introduced Startup Weekend to Taiwan and ran the MobileMonday Taipei chapter. In these early years Yushan also made a handful of direct angel investments of its own capital into Taiwanese startups — most since wound down; Lucent Sky, a web-security company, remains.

2012–2018

Co-built the APEC O2O Accelerator

Conceived and co-built the APEC O2O accelerator with Taiwan's government — running startup programs around the world, from Nanjing to Lima.

2015

Volkswagen Group, Audi & Coca-Cola

Became technology scouting partner to the Volkswagen Group and Audi — and introduced ProLogium, the Taiwanese solid-state lithium-ceramic battery maker, to Audi. Ran a China scouting engagement for Coca-Cola; toured 10 cities with GSMA on "The Rise of Asia's Innovation Machines".

2018–2020

Venture partner & ABB / Siam Cement scouting

Venture partner to HeidelbergCement's Foundamental fund; scouted vision tech in Japan for ABB's YuMi robot and high-temperature sensors for Siam Cement. Founded Mosaic Venture Lab, powered by Audi AG and Taiwan's MoEA.

2026

Yushan rebooted

With Mosaic running independently, the founder returns to Yushan full-time — scouting, investment advisory, and startup growth under one roof again.
